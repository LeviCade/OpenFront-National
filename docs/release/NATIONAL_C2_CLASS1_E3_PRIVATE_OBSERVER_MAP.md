## Authority and transport

E2 remains sole owner of socket authentication, GameServer, GameRunner and the existing Zbin messages:

- `national_start` — sanitized public GameStartInfo + principal clientID + current tick; **no canonical Turn list**.
- `national_projection` — E2's existing `{tick, payload}`, where `payload` is generated by `encodeNationalRecipientProjection` after the E3 `NationalServerRecipientSession.projectGameUpdate` **on the server-owned authoritative Game tick**. Global JSON hard cap: **2,000,000 characters**. The callback is bound to original authenticated `Client.ws`, never `observerId` or tile cursor supplied by a browser.

Existing top-level view fields remain: `tick`, public `nationalPolitics`, public/participant-approved `nationalConflicts`, recipient-only `nationalAccessionOffers` (including `offers:[]` when revoked), actor-only `nationalCommandResults`, allowed `updates` and sight-filtered `packedTileUpdates`, `packedUnitUpdates`, `packedPlayerUpdates` and `packedMissileTimers`.

## Leakage properties and limitations

- **FOG-02:** public empire roster membership/leader is visible; secret pact/coalition terms are never in the DTO. Non-member invitations and other principals' receipts are absent, not masked placeholders. E1's wider separate pact contract must be proven independently before FOG-02 can be considered complete for all pact types.
- **FOG-03:** a foreign border structure inside the _real_ 100-map-tile sight radius may appear; beyond it remains unseen. Border owner markers never reveal an invisible adjacent owner.
- **FOG-04:** E1 current coastline and permitted warship observations extend to 200-map-tile naval radius; only observed ship markers are published.
- **FOG-05:** even an observed enemy transport never includes landingDestination, targetTile, cargo troop count or route. Hidden landing positions remain absent unless independently lawful visible current tile data reveals a map change.
- **Reconnection/revocation:** original socket loses delivery. New original-account socket receives `reset:true`, currently pending own offers and no prior receipt journal. On loss of reciprocal empire sight, the next map packet sets `reset:true`; the browser must clear old ally-derived observations.
- **Paging limits:** one tick does not guarantee full-map coverage. E4 must show partial visibility until `nextCursor:null`, and must not silently invent missing ownership. Presently `unitsTruncated:true` means more sightings exist than 2048 markers; follow-on unit pagination may be required for enormous worlds. This is **not** complete full-map production readiness.
- **Release status:** real GameRunner/source and Zbin in-process evidence is distinct from E5 independent physical browsers and E6 GPU release. Manager-certified project score remains **41%** until official reassessment.

No full Game, GameSnapshot, canonical turns, private WAR state, secret treaty clauses, account credentials, hidden enemy army positions, or unfiltered packed buffers are permitted on the outbound wire.
