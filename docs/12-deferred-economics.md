# 12 — Explicitly deferred: Economic Revolution

**DECISION:** NATIONAL v1 will NOT redesign the underlying monetary/production economy.

We considered an ambitious economic layer with national currencies, real goods, production/consumption, supply chains, economic specialization, price-based construction, GDP/income projections, loans, bonded credit markets, notes, collateral, sovereign ratings and financial institutions. These ideas are **potentially revolutionary but require a separate version**, because their accounting and balancing are too large to combine safely with the initial sovereignty overhaul.

## Out of scope in NATIONAL initial implementation

- Currency issuance, multiple national currencies, FX markets or national monetary policy.
- Goods production chains, commodity inventories, physical industrial input costs or redesigned real-economy trade.
- Sovereign debt instruments and secured lending; promised repayment, collateral liens, foreclosure, tradable notes and lender visibility.
- Reference global rates, local credit risk premiums, debt auctions, central banks or market makers.
- Structure pricing overhaul: no approved removal of discounts and no approved change to flat $1M structures / $3M SAMs.
- Credit ratings as public data (no sovereign credit market yet).
- Gold-for-peace or land-for-peace bargain engine with complex contractual penalty/collateral system.

These concepts remain available as a **future research direction**, not a promise to implement them.

## What still belongs in NATIONAL

- Existing game gold and trade income.
- Peacetime embargoes and secondary sanctions, implemented as actual permissions/trade consequences.
- Physically enforced naval blockades and neutral-ship returns without payout.
- Imperial treasury revenue assignment, taxes, construction permissions, and real troop/gold transfers.
- Intelligent economic decisions using currently available OpenFront economy.
- Diplomacy and intelligence interfaces designed not to depend on gold being the only future currency.

## Future-proofing rule

Keep the diplomacy system coupled to an abstract **trade permission / revenue transfer** interface where practical, not hardcoded assumptions about arbitrary coin denominations. A future economic simulation may change what a trade route carries or what money represents; it should not require rebuilding war declarations, empire sovereignty or treaty voting.

## Research-only future issues

If explored later, insist on accounting conservation, no riskless insurance money from system default, no collateral duplication, proxy abuse protection, and explicit elimination/default consequences. No guarantee of lender repayment without genuine existing assets or real identified payer.

**Do not quietly sneak credit or commodity mechanics into Stage 3 NATIONAL PRs.**
