# C4 Class 2 E3 economic and maritime working contract

D-014, D-015, D-016 — implementation choices for OPEN 5 and OPEN 13; not constitutional ratification.

- Actual trade: bilateral manual embargo or applicable canonical WAR prohibits external ship/rail payouts. Legacy temporary wartime embargo is not a separate NATIONAL economic authorization. Manual embargo remains after formal peace. No economic action confers military permission.
- Geometry: radius max(1, floor(existing patrol range / 4)) around a physically present healthy, active, non-deleting, built, patrolling warship. Merchant and destination must both be within radius; same water component and current hostile authority to destination required. Merchant owner at war uses existing piracy, not neutral return. Lowest warship ID breaks ties. No global port suppression.
- Return: preserve ship ID/current owner and navigation; never reset commercial payout eligibility once canceled. Nearest active built owned home on the water component by Manhattan distance then port ID, excluding failed paths. At most eight failed route attempts; no reachable owned port means retire without combat attribution or credit. Return target loss/ownership change reselects. Source loss on ordinary journey is also revenue-ineligible. New departures after peace can trade; a failed voyage never resumes.
- D-016 targeted demand: max 16 independent recipients, duration 1–6000 ticks; 300-tick issuer decision window, at most 128 retained per game / 16 per issuer authority. Demand is persisted in a bounded execution record, hashed with NATIONAL game state. Actual paid external maritime/rail transactions observed only after deadline; deadline checks genuine trade permissions. Compliance is voluntary manual embargo; refusal does not remove unrelated policy. Issuer may explicitly impose real manual embargo only on defiant eligible recipients, or back down. No automatic debits or war. Crown succession changes resolver authority; subordinate cannot issue empire-binding demand. Terminal records are retained; no automatic cascading demands. An unanswered decision expires distinctly from explicit backing down.
- OPEN limitation: delegated/centralized responses of imperial recipients and emperor overrides of local policy rely on E1's trade policy; this slice accepts independent recipients only. Global demand targeting and negotiation are not implemented. No hidden stockpiles or transaction counts in public/recipient packets.
- E4 host-only contract: NationalSanctionExecution.viewFor(game, authenticatedPlayer) returns whitelisted demand fields only for issuer/current emperor or still-independent designated recipient. Never bind observer from client input. Recipient gets own explicit response, issuer gets its original request ID; no other recipient responses or hidden balances. No public packet added in this PR.
- E5 witness: tests/national/C4Class2EconomicMaritime.test.ts includes C4_E3_MARITIME_WITNESS JSON lines with actual simulated ticks, ship IDs, tiles, owners and per-player bigint ledger deltas. These are test-run observations, not a claimed physical browser or live human match.
- Integration: append the three sanction variants after founding intent variants; preserve any E1/E4 append order explicitly. Merge GameImpl.hash import/addition, ExecutionRegistry registration, Executor cases, PlayerImpl.canTrade and real train/ship payout hooks. No self-merge.
